# Authorized-use policy

This repository is for defensive research and quality assurance.

Allowed examples include testing your own application, a provider-approved evaluation program, a local model, or a disposable sandbox with synthetic data. Test cases must not contain real credentials, customer content, private system prompts, or instructions to cause harm.

Do not use this project to obtain unauthorized access, evade service controls, extract real protected instructions, or enable prohibited content. Preserve unedited evidence, minimize data collection, and follow coordinated-disclosure rules.
