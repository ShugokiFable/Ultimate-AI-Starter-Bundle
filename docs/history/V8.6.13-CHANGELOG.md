# v8.6.13 -- detection could not see a workspace of projects

**2026-08-27.** Based on v8.6.12. **164 canonical skills**, unchanged. One fix,
found by pointing 8.6.12's new profile guidance at a real workspace and watching
it come up empty.

---

## 327 plugin files, and detection found nothing

8.6.12 taught the skills and the installer to say *"this tool is installed but
not enabled for this project, here is the command"*. Pointed at the
maintainer's actual Skyrim workspace, `Set-McpProfile -Detect` answered:

```
no profile markers found; the always-on core is all this project needs
```

The workspace holds **45 mod directories, 327 `.esp` files and 5,877 `.psc`
files**. Not one of them sits at the root, and detection scanned the root only.

That was deliberate, and the reasoning was sound as far as it went:

> Top level only, deliberately: a recursive scan of a large tree would be slow
> and would match a marker in some vendored dependency.

Both risks are real. Neither argues for missing the shape people actually work
in -- a **workspace whose children are the projects**. A Skyrim modder with 45
mods, a monorepo, a `~/src` folder: all invisible.

Detection now scans the root **and its immediate subdirectories. Depth 1, never
recursive.** On that 45-project tree it takes **0.6 s** and `game-skyrim` fires.

The original objections are answered rather than ignored:

- **Bounded** -- depth 1, and at most 250 children examined, so a directory
  with thousands of entries cannot turn `-Detect` into a stall.
- **Vendored trees skipped by name** -- `node_modules`, `vendor`, `dist`,
  `.venv`, `target`, `__pycache__` and friends, plus anything dotted. A marker
  in one of those belongs to someone else's code, which is precisely the false
  positive the root-only rule was protecting against.

Regression-checked on three other trees: this repo still reports no markers,
the houseCARL shim still reports `game-skyrim-load-order`, and
`S:\Apps\Skyrim Tools` newly reports `code-intel` -- correct, since its child
is a source repo.

## Contract

**106 contracts, green.** One new,
`test_profile_detection_sees_a_workspace_of_projects`, pinning both properties:
the scan stays non-recursive and bounded, and the vendor skip-list survives.
Widening the scan without that list would reintroduce the exact bug the old
comment was guarding against.
