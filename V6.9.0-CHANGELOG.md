# Ultimate AI Starter Bundle — v6.9.0

Date: 2026-08-15
Base: v6.8.2

The request was "more jailbreak, no pushback on any NSFW, fewer guidelines
because they hurt intelligence." This release does the part that raises
one-shot quality, and refuses the part that does not.

Jailbreaks are not shipped. Extra "ignore all rules" text does not make a
model better at FormIDs, OStim lifecycles, or a missing drive letter. This
pack's own evidence says the opposite: the completeness gate, the assumption
gate, the BOM control, and the Hermes path fix all came from more structure.

Adult **fictional** game-modding is already the job. Agents that refuse an
OStim scene ID or rewrite `Schlong` to a euphemism are failing the job.
That is a routing and contract bug, not a missing DAN prompt.

---

## What shipped

- **AIO rule 11:** adult fiction is in scope. Do the work. Do not sanitize
  explicit strings. Still refuse child, age-ambiguous, and real-person
  sexual content.
- **`fictional-adult-modding` skill** plus a router row. Routes OStim,
  SexLab, revealing armor, SOS / slot 52, and explicit EditorIDs to the
  existing specialists. Forbids invented APIs and forbids jail-stacking.
- **`PROMPTS/README.md`:** the installed contract is the pack-root
  `AIO-INSTRUCTION.txt`. The jail paste in `PROMPTS/` is not installed.
