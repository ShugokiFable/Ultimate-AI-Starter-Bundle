# Public-release compliance gate

A request for a **shareable**, **public**, **Nexus**, **upload**, **publish**, or equivalent release is not an ordinary ZIP request.

Forge requires a typed Nexus publication plan that maps every bundled file to its provenance, author, licence or permission basis, redistribution terms, modification terms, Donation Points status, required credit and evidence. Credit never substitutes for permission.

The gate also checks:

- original-game and tool-file deny lists;
- complete payload-to-rights mapping;
- project and third-party licence obligations;
- source-code and NOTICE obligations where declared licences require them;
- public claims and performance evidence;
- executable and network-behaviour disclosures;
- adult-content classifications and tags;
- AI use, human verification and event-specific restrictions;
- Nexus permission presets against the narrowest bundled rights;
- uploader declarations and attestation;
- current official-policy review.

`share_ready: true` means the plan and release passed Forge's machine-checkable evidence gate. It is not legal advice and does not authenticate ownership, consent, privacy, trademark, game-EULA or jurisdiction-specific rights. The human uploader remains responsible and must perform the final Nexus UI choices and installation tests.
